## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon judges reviewing a working InfraSight prototype. They need to understand the anomaly signal, inspect its evidence, and see its limits quickly.

### Brand Personality
Technical utility. Calm, credible, precise. The interface should feel like an analyst's working document, not an alarm dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-only editorial minimalism: warm paper canvas, ink-like typography, exact dividers, restrained semantic color. Avoid gradients, glowing effects, pill-heavy UI, decorative charts, and generic SaaS cards.

### Design Principles
- Evidence sits beside priority; no score appears without context.
- Use typography, whitespace, and fine rules before color or decoration.
- Keep controls and states plain, legible, and task-focused.
- Preserve the human-review disclaimer in product language.
- Support reduced motion and keyboard navigation.
